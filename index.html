About the magazine

A bridge between Arab and Asian business since 1982

Arab Asian Trade (AAT) is a monthly economic advertising magazine founded in Taiwan in 1982 by Mr. Mohammad Marwan Fattal, as a step towards building awareness in trade, industry and technology transfer.

We cover trade and industrial exhibitions with research and analysis, and write about what matters to our readers in manufacturing, technology transfer and new developments in trade, industry and innovation.

For more than four decades, AAT has connected suppliers and manufacturers in Asia with buyers in the Arab world, Africa and the rest of the world, contributing to the success of many of its clients and readers.
