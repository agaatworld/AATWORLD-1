4) OUR BRAND KIT (use exactly)
====================================================================
COLOURS — rule 60 / 30 / 10
- Navy #0F1B2D (RGB 15,27,45) — backgrounds, 60%
- Steel blue #2E4A68 (RGB 46,74,104) — rings, secondary panels, 30%
- Amber #F2A541 (RGB 242,165,65) — lines, arrows, frames, 10%
- Warm white #F4E9D8 (RGB 244,233,216) — all text
- Orange #FF8A3D (RGB 255,138,61) — «عز» and the ONE highlighted word only
- Muted grey for small labels/source: #A8B3C1
- Contrast on navy: text 14.4 · amber 8.4 · orange 7.4 (all pass WCAG)
- Forbidden: long red or orange text · more than 2 highlighted words per design/caption · pure white on pure black.

FONTS
- Titles: Thmanyah Serif Display (Bold/Black)
- Body, captions and «الثبات عز»: Thmanyah Sans (Medium/Bold/Black)
- Numbers keep left-to-right direction inside Arabic lines.

LOGO
- Round logo, one corner only, never stretched, never recoloured. Small corner badge on videos.

5) CHANNEL RULES (fixed)
====================================================================
1. Every video is checked for music; any music is removed and only voices (speech/nasheed vocals) stay.
2. Any landscape video becomes square 1:1 (1080×1080): video in the middle, the empty space designed in the channel style.
3. Captions inside the Reels safe zone: away from top 270 px, bottom 420 px, right 120 px (on 1080×1920); word-by-word with the voice; strong words get an effect (slam/glow/punch), 1–2 per line.
4. Main person always in the centre of the frame.
5. Rings draw themselves at the start, then rotate slowly with the speech; arrows enter and move with each new line.
6. Outro transition smooth (~0.6 s cross-fade), no frozen frame. Intro/outro: lens/focus effect, calm sound (camera, wind, air — no loud bass/drums, nothing repeated), hold 1–2 s, then fade to black.
7. Source always credited clearly at the bottom, no extra empty space under it.
8. Never show other channels' end cards, handles or logos.
9. Covers use «كلنا مقاومة».
10. When a nasheed is added: choose a strong part that fits the meaning (vocal only, no instruments).

7) WHAT TO ASK FOR IN THE PROMPT (checklist)
====================================================================
- Output sizes: Reel 1080×1920 (safe zone respected), post 1080×1350, square 1080×1080 for landscape sources.
- Remove music (Demucs or similar), keep voice; loudness −14 LUFS.
- Stabilise shaky phone footage; no extra sharpening on AI-upscaled clips.
- Word-by-word captions synced to the transcript above, Thmanyah Sans, strong words with one effect.
- Motion graphics: rings that draw then rotate slowly, arrows per line, subtle particles; calm, not flashy.
- Intro/outro from the brand kit; smooth 0.6 s transition; no frozen frame.
- Credit the source; remove other accounts' watermarks/end cards.
- Use real photos, not AI figures, for real people (martyrs especially).
