# Concept "The Trade Floor" (slug: exchange)

A working tool for buyers. The home page is a board of what an importer needs this week: a search field, the nearest shows, the factories' products moving on a conveyor, the factory directory, the current issue. Everything is a list, a board or a grid with big, readable rows; red is kept for the places where the visitor acts; the rest is navy ink, white boards and cool grey floor, like a port's departure board and a customs ledger.

Reference captures of today's site (Arabic unless noted): `ref-home-ar.png`, `ref-home-ar-full.png`, `ref-club-ar.png`, `ref-products-ar.png`, `ref-exhibitions-ar.png`, `ref-flip-ar.png`, `ref-home-en.png`. Side by side: `ref-vs-new.png`.

Today's structure, in one paragraph (so the concept departs from it on purpose): one floating white pill menu with eight content types (magazine, articles, news, shows, products & modern industries, club, about, contact) over a long marketing scroll: a moving shelf of mixed article/product cards, a red-gradient "45 years" hero with the cover, a dotted trade map, a logo strip, twelve dark sector tiles, products, articles, news, show rows, a dark contact box, a dark-red footer. Inner pages open with a red-gradient banner and alternate cream/white bands of rounded cards with soft shadows and pill chips. The magazine is a separate full-screen flipbook with a side drawer. Company pages exist but are reached only through the magazine or a product.

## 1. Difference statement

1. **Navigation.** Today: one centred floating pill menu of 8 content types; phone: hamburger. New: a two-tier working header: a white tool bar (red logo at the start, an always-visible search field, a red "تواصل معنا +886 966 404681" button, language switch + globe) and a navy rail of 8 sections with a red "you are here" underline. On phones the rail becomes a fixed bottom tab bar (الرئيسية · معارض · مصانع · منتجات · المجلة) with the rest under the ≡ button.
2. **Home page.** Today: a marketing scroll (shelf → red "45 years" hero → map → logos → sector tiles → products → articles → news → shows → contact). New: a buyer's board: search + four live counters + the current issue with a red stamp, then the nearest-shows board, the product conveyor, the factory directory, the issue index, news & articles, the club, contact. The visitor can find a show, a factory or a product without leaving the first screen.
3. **Grouping of content.** Today: by media type (magazine, articles, news, products, shows, club). New: by what a buyer is looking for: **Shows**, **Factories** (a directory of the 35 advertisers, today hidden behind the magazine), **Products** (advertisers' products, with the modern industries as a second board on the same page), **Magazine** (reader + issue index), **Business Club**, **News & articles** (one reading page, two lists), **About** (about + advertising positions together), **Contact**.
4. **Layout language.** Today: rounded cards, red gradients, soft pink shadows, pill chips, cream bands. New: a flat ledger: 4px corners, 2px rules, boards (tables) with a navy header row, white and cool-grey bands separated by a 4px ink rule, no shadows (only the fixed assistant button), one square red marker before each section title.
5. **Shows.** Today: big flag cards ("choose the country"), then field chips, then rows. New: one board with Date / Show / City / Country / Field columns, a country strip with counts above it, field chips and a search field; month-only shows carry a yellow highlighter mark ("يناير 2027 · الشهر فقط") instead of a fake day.
6. **Typography.** Today: Al-Jazeera Arabic (rounded, friendly) and an Inter-like Latin. New: **Readex Pro** for Arabic and Latin text (a face designed for readers with low vision: wide counters, open shapes) and **Archivo** (a signage grotesque with a width axis) for English headlines, big numbers, dates and show names; tabular numerals everywhere.
7. **Colour.** Today: red gradients on every opening, dark-red footer, cream bands, coloured card gradients. New: deep navy ink for authority (opening board, nav rail, footer, titles), white boards on a cool grey floor, and red only where the visitor acts or is (buttons, the active tab, the logo, stamps, markers). A yellow highlighter is the one extra mark, used only for "this month" dates and search hits.
8. **Opening.** Today: a red gradient hero with "45 عاماً" and the cover. New: a navy board with the headline "اعثر على معرض أو مصنع أو منتج في ثوانٍ", a 76px search field, the four counters (1,464 shows · 35 factories · 127 products · 2,831 members) and the current issue with the red "العدد 560" stamp and "اقرأ العدد / PDF" buttons.
9. **Magazine.** Today: a separate full-screen flipbook with a drawer. New: the reader lives inside the site frame; the issue index (13 articles, 45 advertisers with page numbers) is a list beside the pages; tapping an ad opens the factory's panel, never another page.
10. **Factories.** Today: company mini-sites reachable from the magazine and products. New: a first-class **Factory directory** (دليل المصانع): a grid/board of all 35 advertisers filtered by country and field, each with its real logo, city, field, number of products and magazine pages, and the contact facts.
11. **Feel.** Today: a warm magazine brochure. New: a trade terminal: departure board, ledger rows, stamps, counters. Utility first, authority second, decoration never.
12. **Motion.** Today: reels, a shelf, hover lifts, gradients. New: only the product conveyor moves (slowly, pauses on hover); nothing else animates.

The owner sees the difference in three seconds: navy instead of red gradient, a search field instead of a slogan, a table of shows with dates instead of cards.

## 2. Identity

- **Logo.** The red script logo "AAT · Ideas For Business" (`img/brand/aat-logo-red.svg`) sits at the start of the white tool bar, 60px tall on PC, 44px on phone, always on white, never on navy or red. The white version (`aat-logo-white.svg`) closes every page in the navy footer at 64px. Favicon and app icon: the white logo on a solid red (#D21F26) square (32/48/180px); at 16px it reads as the red AAT square. The tagline is only ever shown inside the logo and as the last line of the footer ("AAT · Ideas For Business · www.aatworld.com"); it is never typed as a headline.
- **Motif 1, the departure board.** The nearest-shows board and every list on the site is a table with a navy header row, tabular dates in the first column, flags, and a chip for the field: the way a port or airport board tells a traveller where to go. Dates, counts and page numbers are typeset in Archivo with tabular numerals.
- **Motif 2, the stamp.** A rotated (4°) red outlined box in bold capitals/bold Arabic: "العدد 560", "في العدد 560 · صفحة 52", "عضو النادي". Used once per board at most: on the current issue, on a factory's page to show where its ad is printed, on the club page. It is the publisher's own stamp, not a decoration.
- **Motif 3, the highlighter.** A yellow mark (#FFE873) behind a date or a word, like a pen on a printed list: month-only show dates, search hits in a result list, the current month in the month strip.
- **What makes it unmistakably AAT:** the red logo at the top and the white logo at the bottom of every page; the navy + red pairing, which is the pairing of the printed cover of issue 560 (blue frame, red masthead); the magazine issue stamped on the home page; the owner's exact wording in the rail ("معارض دولية", "نادي رجال الأعمال", "مجموعة التجارة العربية الآسيوية" in the footer).

## 4. Typography

- **Arabic: Readex Pro** (Google Fonts), weights 400/500/600/700. Designed for readability including low-vision readers (the Arabic sibling of Lexend): wide, open letterforms, generous counters, no thin strokes, strong at 18px and still clear at 16px. It also carries the Latin text of the Arabic site, so an Arabic line with a Latin show name reads as one voice.
- **Latin display: Archivo** (variable, width 75–125, weight 500–900). A signage grotesque: English H1/H2, the big counters, dates, page numbers and show names on the boards (set at 92–95% width, weight 700–800). It is the "departure board" voice.
- **Latin body: Readex Pro** 400/500 (English paragraphs, UI labels, buttons on the English site).
- **Scale (LARGE MODE).** Body 18px/1.7 (Arabic 1.75), minimum for any text 16px (secondary lines 16–17px), nav rail 20px/600, buttons 20px/700 (phone 18px), chips 16–18px/500, board rows 19–21px, counters 44px/800 (phone 34px). H1 48px/1.3 Arabic, 56px/1.1 English (phone 32 / 38); H2 38px/1.25 Arabic, 42px/1.1 English (phone 30 / 34); H3 24px (phone 20). Arabic headings line-height 1.25–1.35, Arabic body 1.7–1.75, Latin display 1.1.
- **Numerals.** Western digits everywhere, including the Arabic site (as today); thousands with a comma (1,464); dates and counts in Archivo with `tabular-nums` so columns line up; day ranges "9–17" isolated as LTR so the order never flips.

## 5. Layout language

- **Grid.** Content width 1760px with 32px gutters (16px on phones), 12 columns, 16px gaps; boards span the full width; the factory grid is 6 columns on PC, 4 at ≤1500px, 2 on phones.
- **Rhythm.** Sections are 48/56px tall bands alternating paper and floor. Every section begins with a 4px ink rule across the width, then one title row: an 18px red square, the H2, and under it the count line in slate (data, not a kicker), with the section's one action button at the end side. No kicker labels, no duplicated titles, no "see all" links in the body; one button per section.
- **Boards (tables).** 2px ink frame, 4px radius, navy header row 56px with white 18px labels, rows ≥ 76px, zebra #F8FAFC, floor on hover; each row is one link. Columns: Date 230 · Show 1fr · City 200 · Country 220 · Field 280. Under 1180px the header hides and each row becomes a two-column block; on phones one column (date, name, city, country, field stacked).
- **Cards.** White, 2px rule border, 4px corners, no shadow; hover = ink border. Product tile 300×(210 picture + name + company); factory tile 6-col with 92px logo area; companies without a logo show their name large in the logo area, never a placeholder.
- **Buttons.** 56px min height, 20px/700, 24px side padding, 4px radius: red fill (primary), ink fill (section actions), ink 2px outline (secondary), white outline on navy. Phone 52px/18px.
- **Chips / filters.** 2px rule, 4px radius, 16–18px/500–600, ≥48px tall with a flag or count pill; selected = ink fill with white text. Country strip: flag 30×21 + name + count.
- **Images.** Logos and products `object-fit: contain` on white in a ruled frame; photos `cover` with a 1px rule; flags 30×21 with a 1px rule; the cover with a 1px rule. Items without a shipped picture show no picture box.
- **Blue** only for web/e-mail addresses on light backgrounds.

## 8. RTL

- `dir="rtl"` on the root for Arabic; all spacing and positions in logical properties (`inset-inline-end`, `padding-inline-start`), so the English, Chinese and Malay versions mirror automatically: logo at the start, action buttons at the end, red tab underline, red square before the title.
- Latin runs inside Arabic (show names, venues, company names, product names, phone, e-mail, address, URLs) are wrapped in `<bdi dir="ltr">`; day ranges "9–17" are isolated as LTR; product tiles are whole LTR blocks because their content is Latin; Latin sub-lines in RTL tiles are LTR with end-aligned ellipsis, so the start of a name is never cut.
- Western digits throughout; Arabic month names (يناير … ديسمبر) in Arabic, short English months in English.
- The address is always English and LTR, even in Arabic (owner's rule). The group name appears in all three scripts in the footer with each run isolated.
- Arrows/chevrons are drawn with CSS and flip with `dir`; ticks never flip.
- The English site uses Archivo for headlines (Readex Pro stays for text), so it is a first-class version with its own display voice, not a translated Arabic page; Arabic words on the English site (the group name) are `<bdi dir="rtl">`.

## 9. Motion

- **Moves:** the product conveyor (70s linear loop, duplicated track, pauses on hover/touch, stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Never moves:** the opening board, counters (no count-up), boards, cards (no hover lift, colour change only), the stamp, the assistant button, page transitions (instant), the reader (page change is a swap, no flip animation; the flip may come back only if the owner asks).

## 10. Why the owner will love it

1. **"LARGE MODE"**: nothing under 16px, body 18px, board rows 19–21px, buttons 20px, counters 44px, and a typeface built for weak eyesight (Readex Pro).
2. **"Where are the section separators?"**: every board starts with a 4px ink rule, one red square, one title, one count, one button; bands alternate white and grey.
3. **"Either the date is there or not"**: a show has a day range, or a highlighted month with "الشهر فقط"; never "to be announced".
4. **"Why is it not moving any more?"**: the product shelf is a real conveyor with the real logos, and it is the only thing that moves, so it is noticed.
5. **"Use original logos" and his wording**: 29 real logos on the home page, none redrawn; "معارض دولية", "نادي رجال الأعمال", "مجموعة التجارة العربية الآسيوية" exactly; the address in English; blue only on addresses.
