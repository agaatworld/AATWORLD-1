Symbol

The Meeting Swash

A sign without letters, taken from the curl at the end of each A. Two swashes reach toward each other and turn into spirals: one for the Arab world, one for Asia. The spiral also recalls the cloud scrolls of Chinese art and the flourishes of Arabic calligraphy, so it speaks to both readers of the magazine.
